
### Tournament Bracket Glossary

| **Term (EN)**            | **Term (PT)**                    | **Description (EN)**                                                                                                                    |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Tournament**           | Torneio                          | The overarching competition entity that defines the static rules, name, sport/game type, and general configuration.                     |
| **Tournament Session**   | Sessão do Torneio                | A specific execution or edition of a tournament held during a given time frame, managing registered teams, status, and active progress. |
| **Bracket**              | Árvore de Torneio / Bracket      | The structural or tree representation showing all match pairings and the progression path toward the final winner.                      |
| **Single Elimination**   | Eliminação Simples               | A tournament format where a team is immediately eliminated from the competition upon losing a single match.                             |
| **Double Elimination**   | Eliminação Dupla                 | A format where a team must lose two matches to be eliminated, typically split into an Upper (Winners) and Lower (Losers) bracket.       |
| **Round**                | Ronda / Fase                     | A distinct stage of the tournament consisting of a set of concurrent matches (e.g., Quarter-Finals, Semi-Finals).                       |
| **Match**                | Jogo / Partida                   | A individual head-to-head contest between two participants within the bracket to determine who advances.                                |
| **Participant / Team**   | Participante / Equipa            | The competing entity (individual player or team) registered to play in the tournament.                                                  |
| **Seed / Seeding**       | Cabeça de Série / Emparelhamento | The initial ranking or placement assigned to teams prior to the tournament, used to determine first-round pairings.                     |
| **Bye**                  | Bye (Isenção de Ronda)           | An automatic advancement granted to a team in the first round when the total number of participants is not a power of 2.                |
| **Round of 16**          | Oitavos-de-Final                 | The elimination round consisting of 16 teams competing across 8 matches.                                                                |
| **Quarter-Finals**       | Quartos-de-Final                 | The elimination round consisting of 8 teams competing across 4 matches.                                                                 |
| **Semi-Finals**          | Meias-Finais                     | The penultimate round consisting of 4 teams across 2 matches, whose winners advance to the Final.                                       |
| **Final / Championship** | Final                            | The ultimate match played between the last two remaining teams to determine the tournament champion.                                    |
| **Winner**               | Vencedor                         | The participant that prevails in a specific match or wins the entire tournament.                                                        |
| **Runner-Up**            | Vice-Campeão                     | The participant that reaches the Final match but is defeated.                                                                           |
| **Walkover (WO)**        | Vitória por Falta de Comparência | An automatic victory awarded to a participant when the opponent fails to appear or is disqualified.                                     |
| **Bracket Generation**   | Geração do Bracket               | The algorithmic process of calculating required rounds and creating the initial match structure based on registered teams.              |
| **Match Progression**    | Progressão do Jogo               | The automated process of advancing the winner of a match into the designated slot of the subsequent round's match.                      |
